## Getting around

- [Hyperdia / Japan transit planner](https://www.jorudan.co.jp/english/) — for train times
- IC card (Suica/Pasmo) works on most transit but confirm rural bus coverage near Nagano

## Money & connectivity

- ATMs: 7-Eleven and post office ATMs reliably accept foreign cards
- SIM/eSIM: consider an eSIM (e.g. Ubigi, Airalo) for the group before arrival
