Tick things off by editing this file (change `[ ]` to `[x]`), or just use it as a checklist.

## On the mountain

- [ ] Ski/board jacket & pants (waterproof)
- [ ] Base layers (top & bottom)
- [ ] Mid layer (fleece/down)
- [ ] Ski socks (wool, several pairs)
- [ ] Gloves/mittens (+ spare pair)
- [ ] Goggles
- [ ] Helmet (rentable at resort if you don't own one)
- [ ] Neck gaiter/buff
- [ ] Beanie
- [ ] Sunscreen & lip balm (snow glare is real)
- [ ] Hand/toe warmers

## Gear

- [ ] Skis/board + boots (or rent locally — see [Resources](/resources/))
- [ ] Helmet, poles
- [ ] Backpack for the mountain (water, snacks, extra layer)

## Off the mountain

- [ ] Onsen towel (small + large — many onsens don't provide them)
- [ ] Casual warm clothes for evenings
- [ ] Slippers/indoor shoes (many lodges are shoes-off)
- [ ] Swimwear if hitting a mixed-gender onsen with swim-required policy

## Documents & money

- [ ] Passport (check expiry — 6 months validity)
- [ ] Travel insurance details
- [ ] IC card (Suica/Pasmo) or plan to buy one
- [ ] Cash (many smaller places in Nagano are cash-only)
- [ ] Credit card (notify your bank of travel)
